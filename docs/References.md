# Reference review

Retrieved 10 October 2026. Knowledge version: `2026-10-10-v1`.

- L&T Finance: https://www.ltfinance.com/blog/business-loan/project-report-for-business-loan — business-loan report guidance covering business/promoter context, project investment, financing, projections, repayment and supporting records. Guidance explicitly varies by business and lender. Related fixed/current-assets and demand/term-loan pages were fetched within the same lending section after robots checks.
- Fortrisk: https://fortriskconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/CMA-Sample-report.pdf — nine-page reference, conventional operating/balance-sheet schedules and compact year columns.
- Finline: https://www.finline.in/CMA.pdf — sixteen-page reference, dark table headings and clear reporting-unit labels. A sparse/image-only cover is recorded as such; financial text pages were parsed and representative layouts inspected.
- JS Consultancy: https://jsconsultancy.co.in/tools/cma/Sample-CMA-Report.pdf — twenty-two-page reference with wide landscape schedules, actual/provisional/projected labels and assumptions.
- User-supplied CMA Report Template – Business Loans.docx and CMA_New_Loan_Checklist.pdf — reviewed locally. The checklist separates new borrowing from a new business and identifies evidence to collect. Template benchmarks and formulas are not treated as verified universal lending requirements.
- User-supplied Universal PRD Template (Any Product).docx — product-development template only. Its internal governance instructions do not override the user request and its sections are not used for business-loan reports.

Original report layouts use readable tables, repeated headers, financial-year/status labels, units, an assumptions appendix and draft status. No reference-company names, sample figures, logos, signatures or certifications are imported. Raw references remain local/ignored. The manifest records hashes and retrieval outcomes. Embedding ingestion requires configured Supabase and embedding credentials; fetching alone does not populate pgvector.
